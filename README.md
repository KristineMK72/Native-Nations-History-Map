# Native-Nations-History-Map
# 🌿 Echoes of the Land: Indigenous History Story Map

## Project Overview
This interactive web map tells the story of Indigenous presence, migration, forced relocation, and ongoing sovereignty across what is now the United States. It combines a guided narrative with modern tribal locations, historical routes, and contextual region overlays.

Built with **Leaflet.js**, HTML, and CSS. Mobile-friendly with a full-screen map and collapsible story drawer.

## Key Features

* **Tribal Locations:** Approximate locations of federally recognized tribal headquarters (green dots, clustered).
* **Historical Routes:** Forced removal paths (Trail of Tears and related routes), traditional migration teachings (e.g., Ojibwe Seven Stops), and other contextual corridors.
* **Guided Narrative:** Chapters covering deep time, regional cultures, removal, U.S. policy, treaties, and living Nations today. Click any chapter or use Prev/Next; the map flies to the relevant view.
* **Mobile Story Drawer:** On phones the map stays full-screen; tap the Stories button or the panel header to expand/collapse the narrative.
* **Places & Regions:** Selected ancestral sites, historical events, and contextual region overlays with images and sources.
* **Earth-tone Styling:** Warm typography and refined cards for easier reading.

Live: [native-nations-history-map.vercel.app](https://native-nations-history-map.vercel.app/)

## Data Sources

* Basemap: CARTO Dark Matter
* Tribal points: BIA Tribal Leaders Directory (ArcGIS FeatureServer)
* Historical coordinates, trails, and places: Approximated for educational context from public historical records
* Images: Primarily Wikimedia Commons (see individual credits)

Maps and datasets simplify. Nation voices and self-identification matter more than any visualization.
